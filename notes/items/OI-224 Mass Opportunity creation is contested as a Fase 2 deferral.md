# OI-224 - Mass Opportunity creation is contested as a Fase 2 deferral

**Elena Spini declined mass Opportunity creation to a later phase on 07/10.
Marco Montesi did not accept it: on 08/10 he escalated to Sabatino Rinaldi,
asking whether the bulk function has to be evaluated now, because they have
performed such operations recently.**

## The exchange

Marco Montesi's first precisazione on the Business Blueprint (05/10) was that a
**Recall Tutor** opportunity can be generated by the tutor **or massively by
Pienissimo** — for example from a list of contacts who declared interest in a
post-event survey.

Elena Spini answered on 07/10 at 14:57:40Z:

> _"Attualmente non è stato pensato né richiesto un flusso di creazione massiva
> di Opportunità, consiglio di provare ad utilizzare la piattaforma ed
> eventualmente si potrebbe pensare di includere questo nuovo requisito in una
> fase successiva."_

Marco Montesi replied on **2026-10-08 at 15:55:11Z**, in line, in the same
thread:

> _"Coinvolgo anche @Sabatino per capire se possiamo procedere in questa maniera
> o se la funzione 'massiva' è da valutare da subito, visto che ne abbiamo fatte
> alcune di recente."_

## Why it is a row and not a note in the Blueprint

- **A Fase 2 candidate was created in mail with no Fase 2 row to hold it.** The
  07/10 sweep flagged exactly that; a day later the client declined to let it
  rest there, so the deferral is not agreed.
- The decision now sits with **Sabatino Rinaldi**, who has not answered at this
  watermark. He is also the owner of
  [OI-218](OI-218%20Direction%20has%20not%20seen%20the%20Business%20Blueprint%20before%20the%2013%20October%20confirmation.md)'s
  forwarding commitment and of the WooCommerce plugin backlog
  ([OI-216](OI-216%20The%20WooCommerce%20plugin%20flushed%20its%20unsent%20order%20backlog%20into%20Salesforce.md)).
- _"ne abbiamo fatte alcune di recente"_ is a **statement of current practice**,
  not a wish. If bulk creation is how Recall Tutor opportunities are actually
  made today, deferring it removes a working process at go-live rather than
  withholding a new feature.
- It lands in the **Blueprint thread**, the only document a client
  representative has accepted in writing, four days before the 13/10 production
  confirmation — and the Blueprint was confirmed for signature the same evening
  ([the Blueprint note](../The%20Business%20Blueprint%20delivered%20to%20the%20client.md)).

⚠ **No scope, volume or mechanism has been stated by either side.** Whether
"massiva" means a list import, a report-driven action or a bulk button is
unspecified, and nobody has asked.

## What closing it looks like

Sabatino Rinaldi's answer, and then either a Fase 1 requirement with a named
mechanism or an explicit Fase 2 row the client has accepted — not a mail
sentence.
