# OI-122 - An external address requested access to the internal status page

**A personal Gmail address asked for access to the ROMI-internal project status
mirror, and the request is unanswered.**

Notion notified Aurel Mrruku at **2026-09-04 13:49Z** (15:48 CEST in the
notification body): **Stiven Qehaja `stivenqehaja@gmail.com` requested access to
📊 ROMI - PIENISSIMO Project Status**. Notion's own banner on the mail reads
_"Make sure you trust this external user before sharing any content."_

## Why this needs a person and not a default

**The page is the internal surface, not the public one.**
[STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) and
[its Notion mirror](../The%20Notion%20mirror%20of%20the%20project%20status.md) are
**ROMI internal and name people**; `site/` is the sanitised public surface
([docs/publishing.md](../../docs/publishing.md)). Granting this request shares
named individuals, internal risk language and delivery judgements with whoever is
behind that address.

**The identity is genuinely unknown to the record.** The name appears in no
note, no meeting, no tracker row and no Slack membership this sweep touched. Two
readings are open and they point opposite ways:

- **A colleague or new contributor using a personal address.** ROMI has onboarded
  people onto this project recently and clumsily — Rexhina Hysi was still not in
  Clockify for Pienissimo on 4 September, and Aurel Mrruku had to ask Elena Spini
  to add her that same afternoon. A personal Gmail arriving before a work account
  is a very ordinary shape.
- **Somebody outside ROMI who found or was forwarded the link.**

⚠ **Do not resolve this by inference.** A name that looks like it belongs to a
team is not evidence, and the whole point of the internal/public split is that it
is not decided by guesswork.

## What to do

One check by someone who can see the ROMI directory: **is this address a known
person's personal account?**

- If yes — grant if appropriate, and preferably re-issue against their
  `@romicompany.com` account so that access follows employment.
- If no — **decline it**, and treat it as a signal that the page's sharing scope
  is wider than intended.

Either way, **the sharing settings on that page are worth a look**: a request
could only be made by someone who reached the link.

⚠ **Left alone, this expires into ambiguity rather than a decision.** Notion
access requests do not lapse loudly, and an unanswered one is indistinguishable
from a refused one until somebody asks why they never got in.

**No date. Unanswered as of the end of 4 September.**
