## 🟡 Why it is worth a row

Item ids on this project are not internal bookkeeping: **`OI-NN` maps 1:1 onto
the client-facing tracker's row numbers**, and those numbers are cited in
`REQUIREMENTS.md`, in the published artifacts and in the client's own
correspondence ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), Write protocol). A second tracker
with its own numbering is the one thing that cannot be reconciled after the
fact: a `PIEN-12` and an `OI-12` are different items with the same shape.

⚠ **No conflict has actually been observed.** The project may be for ROMI's
internal sprint work, or for a different slice entirely, in which case this row
closes in a sentence.

## What closing it looks like

Elena Spini says what `PIEN` is for and which of the two surfaces is
authoritative for an open item. If Jira is to carry open items, the mapping to
the existing numbers is written down **before** anything is filed under a new
one; if it is not, the row records that and closes.
