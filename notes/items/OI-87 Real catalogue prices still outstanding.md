# OI-87 - Real catalogue prices still outstanding

ROMI is building and demoing on invented prices. Raised again on 2026-08-06,
alongside the requests for the significant-articles list, the events list and
example bundle codes — all owed by the same two people.

This blocks meaningful bundle demonstrations. A demo whose numbers Pienissimo
does not recognise cannot be reviewed for correctness, only for shape, and the
client has already accepted the shape.

It is the second attempt at the same ask: the underlying condition has been
tracked since 16 July as
[OI-42](OI-42%20List%20prices%20in%20UAT%20are%20placeholders.md). Four weeks and
one closing session later, nothing has arrived.

✅ **Delivered — resolved 2026-08-24.** The prices arrived on **2026-08-07**
and sat unread for seventeen days.
[`Prodotti e Bundle.xlsx`](../The%20Prodotti%20e%20Bundle%20workbook.md) carries
a `Lista Prodotti` sheet of **29 articles across 9 events, with a
`Prezzo Listino` column populated on every row**. These are the client's own
list prices, exported from their registry, not ROMI inventions.

⚠ **The values stay in the file.** Never copy a price into `notes/`, the recaps
or [site/](../../site/) — [docs/publishing.md](../../docs/publishing.md). Record
only that they exist.

**This does not automatically clear
[OI-42](OI-42%20List%20prices%20in%20UAT%20are%20placeholders.md).** OI-42 is
about what is _loaded in UAT_, and UAT still holds the `[PLACEHOLDER …]`
numbers. The input arrived; the load has not happened. Until it does, every
price-bearing output stays internal —
[the risk note](../risks/Risk%20-%20placeholder%20prices%20could%20reach%20the%20client.md)
lists what is already published on invented numbers.

**Coverage is not total.** Golden Numbers and Pienissimo Intensive have no
articles and therefore no prices, and no bundle price appears anywhere — the
`Esempio di Bundle` sheet shows composition only, with **no `PACK-93` price**.
So bundles still cannot be demoed at a number the client would recognise, which
keeps [the bundle demo](OI-13%20Bundle%20effort%20estimate%20and%20client%20demo.md)
short of what it needs.
