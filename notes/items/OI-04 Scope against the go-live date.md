# OI-04 - Scope against the go-live date

ROMI's feasibility confirmation: is the current scope achievable against the
dates? Priority is the **ticket warehouse plus attendance**.

The dates, corrected and current. **The milestone column comes from
`Pienissimo_Project Plan.pptx`** (Elena Spini, 10 July, in the Drive project
folder) — read for the first time on 2026-08-14 and **not previously reflected
anywhere in this repository**:

| Date              | What                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ~1 September      | Data import into Salesforce                                   |
| 7–19 September    | Tour, free events, ~90% new audience                          |
| **10 September**  | **Fine sviluppi Fase 1** — development must be finished        |
| **25 September**  | Approvazione Soluzione 1                                      |
| 29 September      | Food Marketing Festival                                       |
| **6 October**     | **Go-live Fase 1**                                            |
| **24 October**    | Fine sviluppi Fase 2                                          |
| 29 October        | Big kickoff event, 1,500+ attendees                           |
| **31 October**    | **Zoho contract expires**; Approvazione Soluzione 2           |
| **9 November**    | **Go-live Fase 2** — then post go-live support                |

Two of these change the picture:

- **Development on phase 1 ends 10 September, not 6 October.** The 6 October
  date everyone quotes is go-live; it is preceded by UAT, fine tuning, bug
  fixing and training. With the team back ~24–26 August, that leaves roughly
  **two weeks of build** — see
  [the compressed calendar](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20whole%20remaining%20build%20lands%20after%20Ferragosto.md).
- **There is a second go-live on 9 November**, after the Zoho contract expires.
  It has never been mentioned in a meeting record, a tracker or a recap.

Daniela Morgese approved the phase 1 / phase 2 timeline on 2026-08-06 — but
[only the dates, not the scope](OI-83%20No%20phase%202%20estimate.md).

**The confirmation itself has never been given.** The item has been open since
the kickoff on 27 May. Meanwhile the two September events both fall **before**
go-live and both are ticketed, on a
[lifecycle that has never run end to end](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20ticket%20lifecycle%20has%20never%20run%20end%20to%20end.md),
and every remaining workstream
[restarts in the last week of August](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20whole%20remaining%20build%20lands%20after%20Ferragosto.md).

The recap's older "phase 1 by end September / phase 2 by end October" framing
predates the 6 October date and should be read as superseded.
