## Why the reply matters more than an acknowledgement usually would

It carries **two** things at once, and the mail does not separate them:

1. **The date.** 21 October instead of 6 October
   ([OI-124](OI-124%20Go-live%20moved%20to%2021%20October%20and%20the%20register%20still%20says%206%20October.md)).
   `CTX-02` and eight other places in the signed register do not move without it.
2. 🔴 **The Fase 2 perimeter.** The mail points the client at **Slide 4** of
   `Pienissimo_Project Plan 2.pptx` and says everything marked Fase 2 is
   _"attualmente fuori perimetro e necessiterà di una valutazione e quotazione
   separata"_. Named on that slide: **GLS shipment notifications**, **Teachable
   video courses**, and **Ordini Pienissimo Pro (SFDC → Zoho Pienissimo Software
   SRL)**.

That third item is the one Pienissimo has disputed since July
([the dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md)).
ROMI flagged it out of contract in three consecutive weekly statuses; Sabatino
Rinaldi and Fabrizio Paganelli maintained at least the Zoho item was in scope and
escalated to Daniela Morgese. **This is the first time it has been put to the
client as a written perimeter with a request to acknowledge it.**

⚠ **A confirmation of "avvenuta lettura" is not the same as agreement to the
perimeter**, and the mail asks for one sentence covering both. If the reply is a
bare acknowledgement, the scope dispute is not closed by it — and it should not be
recorded here as closed.
