# OI-71 - Service start date owned by the Strategist

**Signature is not service start.** Clients queue days to weeks — Marco
Montesi: _"il cliente va in coda e magari mi firma il contratto oggi, ma io in
realtà parto tra una settimana, tra 20 giorni, tra un mese."_

Aurel Mrruku proposed capturing the date at order-line entry. **Rejected** —
Marco: _"non lo possono mettere perché non lo sanno, non ce l'hanno quel
dato."_ The **Strategist** (two people, possibly a single department mailbox)
enters the real start date at activity kick-off.

ROMI builds **both a page banner and an email alert** when the field is empty.
Both were agreed, not one or the other.

The consequence is that a Performance Plus contract has a period that nobody
knows at signature, which matters for the contracts-as-database reporting —
invoiced versus collected per contract, renewal dates, the service-block flag
on arrears — all of which key off dates this item is about.

Neither the field nor the alerts exist yet.
