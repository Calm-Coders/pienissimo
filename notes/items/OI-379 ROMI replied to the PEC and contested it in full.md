# OI-379 - ROMI replied to the PEC and contested it in full

**On 19 November ROMI answered the client's PEC. Angelo Pastorelli, signing as
ROMI's legal representative, rejects both the reconstruction of the facts and the
legal qualifications, reserves a detailed reply for a separate communication,
confirms ROMI will attend the meeting already fixed for Friday 20 November at
14:30 with a calendar of next activities, and states expressly that neither the
reply nor attendance nor continuing to work is acquiescence. He reserves every
right including as to the contractual fees.**

Answers [OI-375](OI-375%20Pienissimo%20sent%20a%20PEC%20contesting%20the%20implementation%20and%20suspending%20the%20go-live.md).
