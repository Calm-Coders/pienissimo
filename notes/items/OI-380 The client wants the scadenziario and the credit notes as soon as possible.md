# OI-380 - The client wants the scadenziario and the credit notes as soon as possible

**The ROMI-Salesforce-Pienissimo meeting of Friday 20 November at 14:30 took
place. It opened badly, with the client's direction angry at having received
ROMI's PEC reply, and ended somewhat better. What came out of it is a demand: the
client wants the scadenziario and the credit notes as soon as possible. Nothing
was settled — everything stays frozen while Angelo Pastorelli and Gianpaolo Motta
decide how to proceed, and the client's direction was called privately after the
call.**

## What the client asked for

> _"cosa e uscito: **vogliono ASAP lo scadenziario e le note credito**"_

🔑 **Two items, both financial, both already known to this project:**

- **Lo scadenziario** — the payment-schedule / ageing view. Its **reverse gear
  was scheduled as release R1 on 14 December** in the Fase 2 roadmap
  ([OI-342](OI-342%20The%20Fase%202%20offer%20V2%20reorders%20the%20releases%20and%20contradicts%20itself.md)).
  🔴 **The client is now asking for it ASAP, and it sits in the phase the client
  claims is already paid for.**
- **Le note di credito** — credit notes. Named in
  [the Fase 2 offer](../The%20Fase%202%20offer.md) and
  [the Business Blueprint](../The%20Business%20Blueprint%20delivered%20to%20the%20client.md);
  ⚠ **no requirement for them exists in
  [the register](../../requirements/pienissimo-requirements.yaml)**, which stays
  at v1.6.

🔑 **Both are Fase 2 scope being demanded under Fase 1.** That is the PEC's third
ground made concrete for the first time: not an argument about a contract annex,
but two named deliverables with "ASAP" attached.
