# OI-83 - No phase 2 estimate

Daniela Morgese approved the phase 1 / phase 2 **timeline**. Sabatino Rinaldi
reported it in session — _"Daniela ha approvato la timeline … l'ha vista tutta,
mi ha dato l'ok, non mi ha chiesto niente."_

And then, of the Pienissimo Pro scope: _"Tutto questo è pienissimo pro, però
**Daniela non sapeva questa informazione qui**, quindi tocca rifare un altro
giro, ma questo giro me lo faccio dopo le ferie."_

**Her approval therefore cannot be read as acceptance of scope or budget.** She
approved dates for a plan whose contents she had not been told.

ROMI will produce the estimate after the holidays, explicitly outside the
current plan. Until then there is no number attached to
[OI-37](OI-37%20E-commerce%20parallel%20flow%20build.md) or
[OI-38](OI-38%20Salesforce%20to%20Zoho%20for%20Pienissimo%20Pro.md), and no
commercial decision can be taken on either.

This is the clearest statement in the whole record of why
[the dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md)
has not closed in four meetings: it has never actually reached the person who
decides.
