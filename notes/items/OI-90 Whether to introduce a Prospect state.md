# OI-90 - Whether to introduce a Prospect state

Pienissimo's internal open-points table of **11 June 2026** asks whether to
introduce a **Prospect** state alongside Lead and Cliente, _"come in Pienissimo
360"_. Owned by Sabatino Rinaldi and Marco Montesi.

**Never answered, and never tracked** — surfaced on 2026-08-14 from
[the client's June requirements document](../The%20client%20June%20requirements%20document.md).

It is not merely a picklist value. The same document wants the opportunity card
to show **status (Lead / Cliente / Prospect) plus the RFM matrix and historical
revenue split between events and software**, and to use that for **manual,
reasoned assignment** of opportunities to tutors instead of round-robin. So
Prospect is part of the assignment model that
[OI-80](OI-80%20Lead%20routing%20queues.md) is still trying to specify — Marco
Montesi owns both, and has delivered neither.

Whether it survives is a real question: the lead/opportunity flow confirmed on
31 July does not mention it, and adding a third contact state late would touch
conversion, reporting and the queue design at once. **Decide explicitly rather
than letting it lapse** — it has been silently open for two months.
