# OI-38 - Salesforce to Zoho for Pienissimo Pro

Orders containing the **Pienissimo Pro** product pass automatically to
**Pienissimo Software SRL**, which is a **separate legal entity** and keeps its
own Zoho instance. The discriminator is the product; additional-services
opportunities are re-routed at source through marketing URLs.

⚠ **Do not build this.** It is the third of the three items in
[the scope dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md),
and the one Pienissimo argues hardest was discussed pre-sale. ROMI owes a
**quotation** for it if it does land with ROMI.

The "separate legal entity" point is the substance, not a technicality: this
project's client is Pienissimo, and the integration serves a different company.
That is why ROMI treats it as out of contract, and it is the strongest argument
available in the dispute.

Note the irony recorded in
[OI-83](OI-83%20No%20phase%202%20estimate.md): the whole Pienissimo Pro question
was never put to Daniela Morgese — _"Daniela non sapeva questa informazione
qui."_
