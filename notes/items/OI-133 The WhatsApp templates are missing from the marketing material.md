# OI-133 - The WhatsApp templates are missing from the marketing material

**Rebecca Marmo's 24 August delivery is described as containing WhatsApp and email
templates. Elena Spini and Fabrizio Mastracci went through all four attachments
live on 8 September and the WhatsApp templates are not in them.**

## What happened

In [Flussi MKT Parte 2](../meetings/2026-09-08%20Flussi%20MKT%20Parte%202.md),
Fabrizio Mastracci raised it while orienting himself in the funnel material:

> _"WhatsApp mi hanno girato niente WhatsApp … io c'ho solo le logiche dei
> segmenti e i funnel e il doc. Il messaggio WhatsApp, se è questo quello che
> avevo capito, non c'è."_

Elena Spini read the covering description back — _"logo pienissimo e template
WhatsApp e email"_ — and both counted **four attachments**, none of which is the
WhatsApp template. Elena Spini confirmed the templates exist on the client side:
**_"Sì, ce l'hanno."_** So this is a **delivery gap, not a scope gap.**

Fabrizio Mastracci will ask Rebecca Marmo directly. Elena Spini's framing:
_"rispondete direttamente, almeno capiscono che si devono muovere, dato che sono
in ritardo su tutto."_

Collected by Elena Spini during the call into a new Drive folder **`02 Marketing`**:

| File | What it holds |
| ---- | ------------- |
| `SEGMENTI FUNNEL BIGLIETTI.docx` | the segment definitions and the tag vocabulary — the substantive one |
| `FUNNEL RICHIESTA ISCRIZIONE TITOLARE.docx` | one line of text; the criteria are inside screenshots |
| `FUNNEL INVIO BIGLIETTO COMPILATO.docx` | **a title and nothing else** |
| two email PDFs | the `[TEST]` previews Fabrizio Mastracci forwarded at 13:00 and 13:01Z |

⚠ **Two of the three funnel documents are effectively empty as text.** Fabrizio
Mastracci said so himself — _"quegli screen spezzati di quel flusso non è che so
tanto parlanti"_ — and is working from the two test emails instead of the
specification. Whatever logic those screenshots carry has **not** been read into
this record.

the first email is being built now.
