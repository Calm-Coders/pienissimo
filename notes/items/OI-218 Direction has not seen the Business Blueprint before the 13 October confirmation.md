# OI-218 - Direction has not seen the Business Blueprint before the 13 October confirmation

**The confirmation to go to production is due on 13 October. Fabrizio Paganelli
told the 07/10 session that direction — Daniela Morgese — has still not read the
Business Blueprint, and doubted the timeline on that ground. Sabatino Rinaldi
undertook to forward it to her and to read it himself, to pre-empt objections
from management.**

## The sequence as Elena Spini set it out

At [the WooCommerce session](../meetings/2026-10-07%20UAT%20Integrazione%20WooCommerce%20e%20Mexal.md):

| Date | What |
| ---- | ---- |
| **12 October**, 16:00–18:00 | e2e test session on the missing sales scenarios — bundle, recall tutor, performance plus, renewals |
| **13 October** | confirmation for the production release, later changes still possible |
| **16 October** | marketing's ticket tests **in production** |
| **21 October** | go-live, Fase 1 ([OI-124](OI-124%20Go-live%20moved%20from%206%20to%2021%20October.md)) |

⚠ **The Gemini notes render every one of these dates in August.** The months are
a transcription error: the go-live of record is 21 October, and Elena Spini's own
calendar invitation of the same day places the e2e session at **Mon 12 Oct 2026
16:00–18:00**, matching the notes' "dalle 16:00 alle 18:00". The October
readings are used here.

## The objection

Fabrizio Paganelli _"solleva dubbi sulla sostenibilità di tale tempistica,
evidenziando che la direzione (rappresentata da Daniela) non ha ancora preso
visione del documento di progetto Business Blueprint."_ Sabatino Rinaldi
committed, before the meeting ended, to send the document to Daniela Morgese and
to read it himself _"per prevenire contestazioni da parte della dirigenza"_.

## Why this is a go-live item and not a courtesy

- **The Blueprint is the only project document a client representative has
  accepted in writing** — Marco Montesi on 05/10, _"direi che torna tutto"_, with
  three precisazioni. See
  [the Blueprint note](../The%20Business%20Blueprint%20delivered%20to%20the%20client.md).
- It was delivered on **02/10**, five days before this session, with a request to
  read it attentively. Fabrizio Paganelli and Sabatino Rinaldi read it the same
  afternoon and rejected two points of a different document.
- The party who has not read it is the one whose approval the 13 October
  confirmation represents. An objection raised after 13 October lands inside the
  window between the production confirmation and marketing testing in
  production.
- ⚠ Daniela Morgese is also the owner of the **WooCommerce logic document** this
  sweep has failed to identify for four runs. She now receives the Blueprint
  instead.

## What closing it looks like

Daniela Morgese confirms she has read the Blueprint, before 13 October, and
either accepts it or states her objections in time for them to be answered
before the production release.
