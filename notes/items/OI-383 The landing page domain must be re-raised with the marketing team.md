# OI-383 - The landing page domain must be re-raised with the marketing team

**Pienissimo declined to customise the Salesforce domain. Nobody told the
marketing team, and the uncustomised domain will show on the public landing pages
and in the community. Fabrizio Paganelli has asked that marketing be told so they
can change the landing-page domain alone. The email sending domain is a separate
thing, was configured in July on the client's own instruction, and is not in
question.**

Raised at the end of
[the 20/11 internal meeting](../meetings/2026-11-20%20INTERNA%20Check%20Report.md)
and argued out in
[the marketing group DM](../The%20marketing%20group%20DM%20is%20a%20project%20source.md)
the same morning, 09:12-09:16 CET.

## The two domains, which are not the same thing

🔑 **Fabrizio Mastracci's distinction, and the reason this matters:**

| Domain | Status | His words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The email sending domain** | 🟢 **Done, July 2026**, on the client's own instruction | _"quello delle email e stato fatto a luglio"_; _"quello delle email e **obbligatorio** perche senza non puo mandare le mail (e come dire che abbiamo montato una macchina e non abbiamo messo le ruote)"_; _"lo abbiamo fatto mesi fa sotto loro indicazione"_ |
| **The landing-page domain** | 🔴 **Not customised, and optional** | _"quello del lp e **facoltativo**"_ |

⚠ He also noted the client had already gone quiet on him once over this — _"dove
tra l'altro mi avevano gia ghostato"_.

## What the client decided, and what nobody did about it

Elena Spini's account on the call: she had asked Fabrizio Paganelli whether he
wanted the default customisation domain replaced with something like a
`pienissimo` domain, and _"you say no no I don't mind"_. 🔴 **She had discussed
that only with the client's administration** — _"ne abbiamo discusso solo con
l'amministrazione"_ — and had not told marketing.

🔑 **The consequence she identified on the call**: the domain will appear on the
**landing pages** and in the **community**, both of which the client's marketing
department owns and neither of which administration speaks for. _"I totally
forgot about marketing actually."_

So Fabrizio Paganelli's instruction, relayed: tell the marketing team, so they
can decide — and **the change available to them is the landing-page domain only**.

🟢 **This is explicitly not a Salesforce domain change.** Elena Spini closed the
point: _"non e cambiando il dominio in Salesforce, e cambiando se vogliono solo
il landing page domain"_. Aurel Mrruku's caveat stands on the record — any
endpoint that must be told about a Salesforce domain change would be affected,
which is why the distinction is worth keeping.

## Why it is not trivial

⚠ **It is a public surface during a formal contestation.** The landing pages are
the marketing deliverable the client is about to be trained on
([OI-378](OI-378%20Marketing%20training%20is%20undelivered%20and%20the%20client%20did%20not%20attend%20the%20final%20test.md),
training set for **26/11** on forms and landing pages), and the Pienissimo Live
forms go live for an event on **24-26 November**
([OI-371](OI-371%20Opportunities%20are%20re-keyed%20from%20Zoho%20while%20the%20web%20forms%20wait%20for%20ROMI.md)).
A domain decision taken after that is taken too late.

⚠ **And whoever raises it has to be allowed to.** Elena Spini is stood down from
client contact ([OI-375](OI-375%20Pienissimo%20sent%20a%20PEC%20contesting%20the%20implementation%20and%20suspending%20the%20go-live.md)),
and she flagged the ask to the marketing team in the DM with _"metto tema dominio
per LP ok?"_ — agreed with a thumbs-up, but internally, not to the client.

## What is owed

- **The marketing team told**, on the record, with the landing-page option
  explained and a date by which an answer is useful.
- ⚠ **The client-side decision not to customise, in writing.** It exists only as
  a recollection of a conversation and there is no mail or message for it.
- **Confirmation of what the landing pages currently resolve to**, before anyone
  is trained on them.
