# OI-382 - WhatsApp is blocked because Meta blocked the client number

**The WhatsApp channel cannot be delivered. Meta has blocked the number, and the
number is the client's. ROMI names it as one of three things outstanding for Fase
1 closure, and as one of the two that do not depend on ROMI.**

## The evidence

Elena Spini's weekly status post in `#tproj-pienissimo`, **2026-11-20 19:00 CET**,
one line:

> _"**WhatsApp resta fermo: il numero e bloccato da Meta, lato cliente.**"_

And in the same post's red flags, grouped with the marketing training:

> _"Chiusura che dipende anche dal cliente: formazione e test finale del marketing
> e **WhatsApp (Meta)** non dipendono da noi."_

🔑 **It is listed as a Fase 1 closure item** — _"Chiusura della Fase 1 (report e
dashboard, formazione marketing, **WhatsApp**)"_ — which makes this the first
time in this repository that WhatsApp is recorded as owed under Fase 1 rather
than scoped in the offer documents.

## What is not known

⚠ **Almost everything.** The single line is the whole of the evidence:

- 🔴 **Which number**, and whether it is the sending number for the marketing
  flows or a separate business number.
- 🔴 **When it was blocked**, and whether anything ROMI built triggered it. The
  marketing flows were completed and the final test was held on 19/11
  ([OI-378](OI-378%20Marketing%20training%20is%20undelivered%20and%20the%20client%20did%20not%20attend%20the%20final%20test.md));
  nothing connects the two, and nothing excludes it either.
- 🔴 **On what grounds**, and whether an appeal or a WhatsApp Business API
  re-verification is open.
- ⚠ **Who at Pienissimo owns it.** _"Lato cliente"_ names no person.

## Where it sits in the record

WhatsApp appears in
[the Fase 2 offer](../The%20Fase%202%20offer.md) and in
[the Business Blueprint delivered to the client](../The%20Business%20Blueprint%20delivered%20to%20the%20client.md),
but ⚠ **[the requirement register](../../requirements/pienissimo-requirements.yaml)
holds no WhatsApp requirement** and stays at v1.6. So a channel ROMI now counts
towards Fase 1 closure has never been written as a requirement, which is the same
defect as [OI-377](OI-377%20Reports%20and%20dashboards%20were%20never%20built%20and%20have%20no%20requirements.md)
on reports, and it is being surfaced in the same week and for the same reason.

⚠ **Pienissimo Live is 24-26 November.** If WhatsApp was meant to carry any part
of the event communication, it will not.

## What is owed

- 🔴 **The number, the block date and Meta's stated reason**, from Pienissimo.
- 🔴 **Whether anything is being done about it**, by whom, and whether ROMI has
  any part in the remedy or merely records the blocker.
- **Whether WhatsApp is contractually Fase 1 at all** — it is asserted as a
  closure item in a status post and nowhere else.
