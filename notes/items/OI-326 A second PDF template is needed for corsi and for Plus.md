# OI-326 - A second PDF template is needed for corsi and for Plus

**R0 needs two contract templates, not one — courses and Performance Plus have
different general conditions. Aurel Mrruku confirmed it and said it doubles the work.
The open point was then deleted from the document before it went to the client,
because it had never been raised with them.**

## What was agreed internally

In [the 3 November internal follow-up](../meetings/2026-11-03%20Follow-up%20Interno.md)
Elena Spini read out a draft open point — _"template e invio: un template o due,
corsi e plus, condizioni generali"_:

> **Elena Spini:** _"non è unico template, sono due perché è uno dei corsi e uno di
> Plus."_
> **Aurel Mrruku:** _"sono due."_ … _"Ma devi capire che devo fare il doppio del
> lavoro."_

## 🔴 And then it was removed

> **Elena Spini:** _"cioè, io farei vedere questo, cioè non ce l'ho mai parlato di un
> template diverso, **io lo toglierei proprio**."_
> **Aurel Mrruku:** _"toglio."_

🔑 **So [the functional analysis](../The%20stage%20sale%20contract%20functional%20analysis.md)
the client received on 3 November at 18:00:14Z speaks of a single _"nuovo template
PDF"_** (RF2), while ROMI's own position is that two are required.

## Why it is open

1. 🔴 **The scope the client is being asked to confirm by 5 November understates the
   work.** OP-3 asks for written confirmation of R0 before development starts; the
   confirmation would cover one template.
2. 🔴 **R0 is 6-7 days for everything.** Aurel Mrruku's historical cost for **one**
   template was _"da 10 a 15 giorni"_, _"2-3 giorni"_ with AI assistance. Elena
   Spini: _"non posso chiedere più i giorni, mi sa che già m'ammazzano."_
3. 🔑 **Neither template can be copied from Fase 1.** Every dynamic pointer resolves
   against the Quote; the Order lacks fields the Quote has. _"Devi prendere il
   contenuto di quello che c'è e devi cambiare tutti i puntamenti dinamici"_ —
   _"va creato nuovo, non è manco modificato quello che c'è."_
4. 🔑 **Choosing between them requires knowing whether the order is a Plus order**,
   which is [OI-328](OI-328%20A%20bundle%20containing%20a%20Plus%20product%20must%20be%20flagged%20as%20a%20Plus%20bundle.md).
   Aurel Mrruku ruled out keying on the template name or on free text.
5. 🔑 **The client owes the content.** The analysis lists as a dependency: _"Fabrizio
   ed Elisa: testi, condizioni generali e revisione del template PDF"_. ⚠ **For one
   template, as written.**
6. ⚠ **No date, because it is not in the document.**
