# The Zoho tutor calendar is not migrated to Salesforce

**The tutors' Zoho calendars will not be imported or synchronised. If the team
wants to work in the Salesforce calendar, existing commitments must be re-entered
by hand as individual Events. ROMI told the client so in writing.**

## The ruling

Elena Spini to Marco Montesi, Gmail `1a17e66e3c2b5772`, **2026-10-27 17:27:02Z**,
cc Fabrizio Paganelli, `amministrazione@pienissimo.com`, Aurel Mrruku:

> _"**Sincronizzazione / Importazione calendario da Zoho:** non è prevista
> un'importazione o sincronizzazione automatica dei calendari da Zoho a
> Salesforce. Se intendete utilizzare il calendario di Salesforce, gli impegni già
> presenti andranno replicati manualmente all'interno di SFDC come singoli
> Eventi."_

## Reading

🟢 **This is the scope line holding.** It is the first time since
[the 26/10 ruling](ROMI%20will%20absorb%20no%20further%20scope%20in%20Fase%201.md)
(_"non abbiamo più margine per assorbire cose in più"_) that a new client request
has been **declined outright** rather than absorbed.

⚠ **It was declined, not routed.** ROMI asked the client on 23/10 to put
out-of-scope requirements through formal Change Requests
([OI-285](../items/OI-285%20ROMI%20asked%20the%20client%20to%20route%20future%20out%20of%20scope%20requirements%20through%20change%20requests.md));
this answer neither invokes that mechanism nor offers Fase 2. The wording
_"non è prevista"_ states the plan, not a process. **The CR mechanism has still
never been applied to anything.**

⚠ **The operational consequence is unowned.** Nothing says how many commitments
exist in the Zoho calendars, who re-enters them, or by when — and the manual
re-entry falls in the same window as the go-live. No open item was raised by
either side.
