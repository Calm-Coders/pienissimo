# ROMI will absorb no further scope in Fase 1

**ROMI's own management closed the door on absorbing further client requests
into Fase 1 and instructed the project manager to push them to Fase 2. It is
the internal counterpart to the Change Request regime the client has not
answered, and it is the first time anyone at ROMI has set a boundary in
writing.**

## The instruction

`#tproj-pienissimo`, **2026-10-26 13:13:44 CET**, Gianpaolo Motta to Elena
Spini, in full:

> _"Ciao Elena, su questo progetto non abbiamo più margine per assorbire cose in
> più, spostagliele alla fase 2"_

One 👍 reaction. ⚠ No reply in the channel.

## Why it carries weight

- 🔑 It comes from **Gianpaolo Motta**, who is the staffing authority on this
  project — Elena Spini's own 24/10 status cites _"23 gg dopo la review staffing
  con Gianpaolo"_ — so this is a budget ruling, not an opinion.
- It answers the pattern
  [OI-285](../items/OI-285%20ROMI%20asked%20the%20client%20to%20route%20future%20out%20of%20scope%20requirements%20through%20change%20requests.md)
  documented externally: ROMI told the client on 23/10 that future out-of-scope
  requirements must become Change Requests, **and the client answered the rest of
  that mail and ignored that paragraph.** Three days later ROMI set the rule for
  itself regardless.
- It lands against a stated overrun already absorbed: the 24/10 status records
  the ticket and open-order migration as _"+3 gg di Aurel"_ taken into Fase 1,
  and the red flag _"ogni settimana cambia qualcosa che era già deciso."_

## What it does not settle

- ⚠ **It is addressed to Elena Spini, not to the client.** Nothing records it
  being said to Pienissimo, so from their side nothing has changed.
- ⚠ **"Fase 2" still has no agreed perimeter.** The 24/10 status puts Fase 2
  planning as _"slittato alla settimana prossima"_ (Elena Spini / Andrea G.), and
  OI-285 already noted the CR request points at a phase with no boundary.
- ⚠ **Retrospective effect undecided.** The instruction is prospective. Whether
  the scope already absorbed — [OI-274](../items/OI-274%20The%20historical%20ticket%20balance%20has%20no%20migration%20path.md),
  [OI-290](../items/OI-290%20Lead%20and%20Referenti%20migration%20data%20is%20owed%20by%20the%20client.md),
  [OI-269](../items/OI-269%20Event%20communications%20must%20be%20differentiated%20by%20ticket%20type.md),
  OI-277 to OI-282 — is re-opened, billed or waived is not addressed.
- 🔑 **It arrives the same day as a new unplanned migration scope.**
  [OI-290](../items/OI-290%20Lead%20and%20Referenti%20migration%20data%20is%20owed%20by%20the%20client.md)
  surfaced in the 11:00 call and Elena Spini mailed the client about it at
  13:33Z — **twenty minutes after this instruction**, without calling it a CR or
  a Fase 2 item. ⚠ Whether she had read it is not established.
