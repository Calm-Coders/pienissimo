# The Zoho Forms mapping workbook

**`Mappatura_Zoho_Forms_Pienissimo.xlsx` is the form inventory
[OI-14](items/OI-14%20Marketing%20forms%20and%20subdomain.md) has been blocked on
since June, carried in the record for months only as "the 100+ form Excel". It
is now identified: Drive id `1KPZ8pwEg3FFyXDdKu-gkXPtI13Yb5fGi`, owner Fabrizio
Mastracci, created 2026-07-24, **last modified 2026-10-08T08:05:35Z** —
two hours before the internal session that agreed where its fields land.**

Elena Spini posted it to the marketing group DM at 08/10 10:09:30 CEST,
addressed to Fabrizio Mastracci: _"per i form"_.

## What it holds

Its own instructions sheet states the scope:

- **Team Pienissimo × Romi**, Zoho Forms account `segreteria5` on `forms.zoho.eu`
- **Extracted 16/07/2026 · 106 active forms · 1,588 fields in total**
- Sheet **`Indice Form`** — the 106 active forms, each with a clickable public
  link and its field count
- Sheet **`Campi Form`** — all 1,588 fields, one per row, grouped by form, with
  label, type and whether it is mandatory
- 🔴 **The yellow columns are ROMI's to fill** — _"Da compilare da Romi: a quale
  campo/modulo del CRM di destinazione mappare ciascun campo"_, as `→ Campo CRM
  dest.` and `→ Modulo CRM`

System and layout fields (sections, descriptive text, dividers) are excluded
because they collect no data. The workbook flags forms **97–98** (`test`) and
**32** (`MD_SOLD_OUT…`) as test or field-less and asks whether to migrate them.

## Why it matters now

- It is the **denominator of the marketing form work**: 1,588 fields to be
  mapped by ROMI, with **13 days to go-live** and the mapping columns
  unfilled as far as the instructions sheet describes them.
- The 08/10 internal session decided **where the mapped fields land** — a
  dedicated marketing-forms section on the Opportunity, colour-marked — but not
  **which** of the 1,588 are in scope. See
  [the session](meetings/2026-10-08%20Internal%20Test.md).
- Matteo Distaso's hidden-field and UTM mapping of 06/10 covered **two
  prioritised forms**. This workbook is the other 104.
- It is the Zoho side of the same question
  [OI-115](items/OI-115%20Tipologia%20Attivita%20values%20and%20its%20move%20to%20the%20quote.md)
  asks about the four Lead picklists.
