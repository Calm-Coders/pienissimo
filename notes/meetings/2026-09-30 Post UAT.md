## 🔑 The send contract, as agreed

1. **The asset is created on order creation, in `Ordinato`.**
2. **Naming is not unlocked until the invoice for the relevant tranche is paid**
   — the client directive from the UAT two hours earlier, carried straight in.
3. The `Event_Invitation__c` link opens the participant page. **On confirmation
   of at least one participant, a boolean flag goes to `true`** — Aurel Mrruku
   owns it (`01:06:17`).
4. **Marketing Cloud queries for records carrying that flag** and sends
   (`01:07:56`).
5. **Each participant receives their own ticket at their own email address** —
   Fabrizio Mastracci, `00:24:47`.
6. **After the send, a flow activity writes a field back on the asset** so the
   ticket is not sent again on following days — Fabrizio Mastracci committed to
   adding it (`00:34:49`).

🔑 **The send is immediate and transactional, not a nightly batch.** Elena Spini
raised the Marketing Cloud credit cost of continuous update queries; Fabrizio
Mastracci answered that a QR ticket needs no branching logic — it is a
transactional email fired as soon as a record is updated with the flag — and the
room agreed, **dropping the nightly processing** (`01:14:05`–`01:16:02`).

⚠ Aurel Mrruku's performance objection — Salesforce updating thousands of records
a day — was answered with **Data Cloud datagraphs and real-time queries**
(`00:36:02`), not with a measurement.
→ [OI-197](../items/OI-197%20The%20ticket%20send%20flag%20and%20the%20Inviato%20asset%20state%20are%20agreed%20and%20unbuilt.md)

## 🔑 Two new asset states

`Assegnato` when the names are entered, and **`Inviato` after the communication
actually goes out** (`01:10:37`). Aurel Mrruku required that `Inviato` be written
**as soon as the record is modified**, not on a later pass.

🔴 **`Inviato` does not exist.** `AssetStatus.standardValueSet` on `DevMain`
`0b6b828` carries `Ordinato · Disponibile · Rinuncia · Assegnato · Utilizzato ·
Non utilizzato · Annullato` — seven values, and this is an eighth.
→ [OI-74](../items/OI-74%20Asset%20state%20machine.md)

## 🔑 The page shows every paid tranche, not one

Elena Spini proposed filtering the participant page by tranche. Aurel Mrruku
objected that one order can carry several tranches and several different events,
so the page must show **all paid tickets across the tranches** (`01:18:41`).
Agreed. Already-completed entries show as `Assegnato`; the uncompleted parts stay
editable (`00:56:50`).

🔴 **This is what [OI-198](../items/OI-198%20The%20asset%20does%20not%20say%20which%20tranche%20paid%20for%20it.md)
needs a field for.** Aurel Mrruku's own action item: _"Aggiungere un campo
sull'asset che specifichi la tranche pagata per consentire un filtraggio corretto
durante il flusso."_
