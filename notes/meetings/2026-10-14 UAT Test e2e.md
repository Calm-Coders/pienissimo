## The rulings

Three `Concordato`, one `Da approfondire`.

### 🟢 `Concordato` — the quote must show list total, discount total and net total

Elisa Migliano asked for three summary rows on the contractual PDF —
**totale di listino, totale degli sconti, totale netto** (`00:44:51`). Aurel
Mrruku committed to delivering it **within a day across all quote types**
(rinnovo, standard and bundle), reflected **both in the PDF and in the portal**
(`00:46:06`), and the totals are to sit consistently in the desktop and mobile
views (`00:55:52`).

🟢 **It was built the same evening.** `8186816` (Rexhina Hysi, 18:14 CEST,
branch `DevMainOpportunityWork`) adds `Quote.Totale_Listino__c` and
`Quote.Sconto_Totale__c` and carries them into `QuotePdf.page`, the
`quoteAcceptancePage` community component and the Quote layout. See
[OI-207](../items/OI-207%20The%20quote%20and%20order%20layouts%20omit%20the%20commercial%20fields%20the%20client%20requires.md).
⚠ **Not in `DevMain`.**

⚠ **This is the quote, not the invoice.** The same three values on the **Mexal
invoice** are [OI-145](../items/OI-145%20Order%20header%20discounts%20are%20removed.md),
which is unchanged: the order tracciato still has no list-price or discount
field.

### 🟢 `Concordato` — a renewal quote cannot be edited; it is deleted and recreated

Elisa Migliano could not modify a saved renewal quote for the Plus products
(`01:36:31`). Aurel Mrruku explained that for bundle and Plus products, which
generate their tranches automatically, a change requires deleting and recreating
the quote (`01:38:14`). Elisa Migliano and Elena Spini weighed the error margin
for a tutor on the phone and **agreed to keep the current behaviour**, reviewing
the operational impact in daily use (`01:42:10`, `01:46:26`).

### 🟢 `Concordato` — renewals carry no system contract, only the quote PDF

> _"per i rinnovi contrattuali non si prevede un contratto informatico di
> sistema, ma si utilizza esclusivamente il PDF del preventivo."_

Elisa Migliano stated it and Elena Spini agreed (`01:56:41`): the internal
contract must follow the company's own tables, so only the quote PDF is sent.

### 🔴 `Da approfondire` — the approval workflow for tacit renewals

## The chain, step by step

**Opportunity (`00:05:32`–`00:12:16`).** An activation Opportunity for
Performance Plus on the `pienissimo srl` account. The name is free text; the
close date defaults to **5 days** from creation and tutors may change it by hand
against deadlines or events. 🔑 **The agent on the quote is inherited from the
account at creation and stays independent of later changes to the customer
record** — stated in the room, and the confirmation
[OI-214](../items/OI-214%20The%20Mexal%20order%20send%20requires%20an%20agent%20code%20WooCommerce%20orders%20lack.md)
was waiting for.

**Products and categories (`00:13:30`–`00:22:53`).** A **mismatch in the codes
attached to category `C10`** for activation surfaced during product selection.
Aurel Mrruku noted that changing categories on products already inside active
offers creates logical problems, because activations need the digital signature
and renewals do not; Elisa Migliano agreed the constraint is functionally right
and specified that **anagrafica fields such as levels 1 to 4 are managed
directly on Salesforce**. 🔑 **The cause was found and fixed the same day** — the
article sync had never written `Product2.Categoria_Articolo__c` at all; see
[the article sync note](../objects/The%20Mexal%20article%20sync%20to%20Product2.md),
which is Aurel Mrruku's own record.

**New product codes and tranches (`00:24:04`–`00:40:46`).** To get past the
category blocks Elisa Migliano created `plus_N05` and `plus_N06` **on Mexal**.
Permission problems on Salesforce followed; Aurel Mrruku updated the
administrative profile's permissions and **set the tranche count (four) by hand**
on the new products, after which Elisa Migliano entered the per-instalment due
dates on the quote. 🔑 See
[OI-210](../items/OI-210%20The%20delivered%20article%20registry%20carries%20no%20tranche%20count.md)
— the session used the **tranche-count-field** model, not the one-article-per-
tranche registry.

**Discounts and the PDF (`00:42:20`–`00:46:06`).** Discounts tested both as a
percentage and as an absolute value, with the corresponding amounts computed
automatically. Then the three-totals request above.

**Portal and mobile (`00:48:32`–`00:56:56`).** A test quote emailed to a
reference contact to check message customisation and the customer-portal view.
Aurel Mrruku showed the mobile rendering (simulated on an iPhone 16 Pro in the
browser): the column table becomes vertical blocks. ⚠ **Elisa Migliano raised
acceptance of quotes after the 5-day expiry and it was not resolved** — recorded
in [OI-59](../items/OI-59%20Quote%20workflow%20configuration.md).

**Acceptance, signature, Mexal (`00:58:02`–`00:59:21`).** Acceptance does not
block: the document state updates and a DocuSign mail goes out for signature.
The signed order appears among the related items, and transmission is checked
through the **Maxal Integration status** indicator.

**Anticipay (`01:01:30`–`01:03:00`).** The call **does not run for a San Marino
account**, because it is not in the Italian database and is not handled
automatically. 🔑 **And the call is made only on an account's first order, when
the Mexal code is generated** — deliberately, to avoid needless cost. See
[OI-215](../items/OI-215%20Anticipay%20does%20not%20cover%20San%20Marino%20addresses.md).

**The Mexal order (`01:04:04`–`01:05:26`).** Order **OC 1023**, €20,000. Elisa
Migliano found **no tipo merce and no provvigioni percentage** on it and decided
to report both to Mirko Merendi —
[OI-236](../items/OI-236%20The%20Mexal%20order%20carries%20no%20tipo%20merce%20and%20no%20provvigioni%20percentage.md).

**Deferred mass invoicing (`01:07:56`–`01:09:25`).** One order split into four
invoicing tranches through Mexal's **emissione differita documenti**, with due
dates entered and a filter on the RSM services. A trial print produced
**invoice 10 for €5,000**. ⚠ The due-date range the record gives — _"dall'1/02/2026
al 31/03/2026"_ — is **before the session date and inconsistent with the four
tranches**; read as a transcription artefact, **not** relied on. The invoice's
reference wording must be changed by Mirko Merendi to cite the **order** number
instead of the quote —
[OI-237](../items/OI-237%20Mexal%20invoices%20cite%20the%20preventivo%20instead%20of%20the%20order%20number.md).

**The scadenzario return leg (`01:12:42`–`01:23:29`).** 🔑 **This is the first
time any source records the return leg running on a real invoice.** The €5,000
invoice was posted automatically against customer **50171** with **bonifico**
payment. Aurel Mrruku recalled the **03:00 nightly job** and gave Fabrizio a
script to force the sync by hand in 2–3 minutes. In Salesforce the amount, the
tranche and the payment state **`non pagato`** were checked, with order totals
of **ordinato €20,000, fatturato €5,000, incassato €0, da fatturare €15,000**.
Elisa Migliano then simulated the €5,000 collection and ran a second sync script
from the console to update the states.

⚠ **This is not a test of [OI-212](../items/OI-212%20A%20Ri.Ba.%20rate%20reads%20as%20paid%20before%20its%20due%20date.md).**
The payment was a **bonifico**, and OI-212 is about Mexal status `E` on the
Ri.Ba. family reading as paid before the due date. A future-dated bonifico
correctly read `non pagato`. **The `E` branch was still not exercised.**

**API limits (`01:27:42`–`01:29:36`).** Checked **on production**:
**3,360,000 requests a month and 112,000 a day**. Each sync cycle costs two calls
(fatture and scadenzario), on top of account creation and Anticipay. 🔑 **Agreed
to run the script periodically, every half hour or hour.** Nothing schedules it —
[OI-239](../items/OI-239%20The%20Mexal%20scadenzario%20sync%20must%20run%20every%20half%20hour%20and%20nothing%20schedules%20it.md).

**The renewal (`01:34:41`–`01:56:41`).** A renewal Opportunity for
`pienissimo srl`, billing and shipping inherited from the account, renewal
product **N07 / plus-06** split into four tranches. For the Performance Plus
tacit renewal the full DocuSign contract is not mandatory: **the tutor can
confirm and accept the quote internally, turning it straight into an order to
invoice without the customer's approval** (`01:52:05`, `01:53:45`). Aurel Mrruku
confirmed that order too had already gone to Mexal.

**Contracts on the account (`01:58:06`).** Elisa Migliano expected two contracts
on the account, activation and renewal. Aurel Mrruku found the contract **is
correctly linked** but **hidden in the tab by the number of earlier orders** —
[OI-235](../items/OI-235%20A%20contract%20is%20linked%20to%20the%20account%20but%20hidden%20in%20the%20tab.md).

⚠ **A browser difficulty opening order details directly** was also recorded
(`01:17:57`), as on 13/10. Preserved; no claim made.
