1. **Bundle assets stay in `Ordinato` until the invoice arrives.** Elena Spini
   confirmed that selling a multi-event bundle generates the matching number of
   assets and holds them all in `Ordinato`. Bundles without tranches go straight
   to `Incassato`, or wait in `Ordinato` for the invoice. For bank transfers,
   Elena Spini required manual administrative verification before the invoice is
   issued.

## 🔑 Competenza dates cannot overlap between sibling editions

The test threw an error because a bundle product was mapped to a campaign dated
2027. Elena Spini asked whether an earlier competenza date could simply be set;
Aurel Mrruku ruled it out — **overlapping competenza ranges are not permitted on
child entities sharing the same parent** (`01:08:59`). Rexhina Hysi remapped the
fourth product onto the correct campaign.

The auto-association of an order to an edition is by **the order date falling
inside the edition's competenza range**, read back by Elena Spini from the
blueprint.

⚠ **Aurel Mrruku raised the year-boundary case** — an offer issued in one year,
paid or registered in the next. Elena Spini's answer is that competenza ranges are
widened preventively. **That is a mitigation by convention, not a rule in the
system**, and it is the same seam the 30/09 UAT opened when it made the mapping
window the competenza dates.
→ [OI-96](../items/OI-96%20Edition%20mapping%20table%20on%20Salesforce.md)
