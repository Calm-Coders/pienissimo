## Why it is not drilled

🔴 **The transcript is unusable.** The call was in Italian; Gemini transcribed it
as English, and what it produced is not a bad transcript but an empty one —
page after page of `"Okay."`, `"See, see"`, `"Foreign speech."` The few content
words that survive are plainly wrong (`"Mic 66"`, `"Jet to FLU6"`, `"Pandra"`,
`"Salesforce part 11"`).

🔴 **The auto-summary inherits the damage and reads as if it did not.** It
asserts, in confident prose, that the call covered _"Food Marketing Festival
campaign planning"_ for September and an October layout campaign, _"Salesforce
account configurations and Excel import procedures"_, _"original lead sources"_
and _"mobile data parameters"_, and it files one next step: _"\[Unassigned\]
Record Contact: Record contact details into Pandra."_ ⚠ **None of that is
corroborated by the transcript it was generated from**, and "Pandra" is not a
thing in this project. **It is recorded here as a description of the artefact,
not as what was said.** Nothing from it has been written into any item note.

⚠ The timing and subject make a reading **plausible** — the call was called to
settle the `ID riga Mexal` problem (Gmail, 12:52Z and 13:08Z: _"Ho mandato
l'invito per la call. Riesci a partecipare adesso?"_), and the import ran to
completion two hours later. **Plausible is not evidence**, and nothing is
recorded on that basis.
