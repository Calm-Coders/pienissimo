# Giuliano Lanzetti - CEO of both Pienissimo companies

**CEO of Pienissimo Srl *and* of Pienissimo Software Srl**, and additionally
R&D Executive of the software company and listed under Formazione on the events
side. Alongside **Daniela Morgese**, who is General Executive of both.

⚠ **He appears in no meeting, no tracker, no recap and no requirement
document.** He surfaced only from the April 2026 org chart, read on 2026-08-14.

That matters for one reason: the project's central commercial problem is
[the phase 2 scope dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md),
which turns on work for **Pienissimo Software Srl** — a company this person
runs as CEO, and which ROMI argues is not this project's client. Every
escalation so far has gone to Daniela Morgese. Whether Lanzetti is the right or
additional escalation path is an open question nobody has asked.

⚠ **First name probably Giuliano, not confirmed.** The org chart gives only the
initial. [The client's June requirements document](../The%20client%20June%20requirements%20document.md)
refers to services _"venduti da Giuliano in diretta"_ which stay commercially
with Pienissimo Srl rather than routing to the software company — a description
that fits the CEO who also appears under Formazione. An older version of the
`drill-meeting` skill listed a "Giuliano Aranzetti (frontman)", which may be the
same person with a garbled surname, or may not be. **Do not write the full name
into a requirement or a client-facing document until someone confirms it.**

⚠ **Gemini names a _"Giuliano Lanzetti"_ among the participants** of
[the 30 October WooCommerce call](../meetings/2026-10-30%20Connessione%20WooCommerce.md),
alongside Aurel Mrruku, Fabrizio Paganelli, Sabatino Rinaldi and Elisa Migliano.

🔑 **This is a lead, not a confirmation, and the note is deliberately not renamed.**
Two reasons:

1. ⚠ **Gemini mangles names throughout this corpus.** The same day's transcripts
   render Rexhina Hysi as _"Regina"_ and _"Regina Ana"_, Anita Aga as _"Anna"_, and
   Mexal as _"Maxal"_. A first name from this source is not evidence of the kind
   that should rewrite a filename and five link targets.
2. ⚠ **The attendance itself is doubtful.** The invite list is Elena Spini,
   Sabatino Rinaldi, Aurel Mrruku, Fabrizio Paganelli and
   `amministrazione@pienissimo.com` — **no Lanzetti**. Elisa Migliano is
   [Pienissimo administration](Elisa%20Migliano%20-%20Pienissimo%20administration.md),
   so she is the likely holder of the `amministrazione@` address, which leaves the
   Lanzetti name unaccounted for by the invite.

So `uncertain: first name` stands. 🔑 **If it is right, the CEO of both Pienissimo
companies sat in a technical WooCommerce test session** — which would be the first
time he appears anywhere in this project's record, and would matter for
[the phase 2 scope dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md).
**One question to a human settles it: who was in the room.**
