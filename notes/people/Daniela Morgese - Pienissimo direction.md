# Daniela Morgese - Pienissimo direction

Pienissimo's decision-maker. Nothing commercial closes without her, and she
signs the requirements document for the client side.

Her title on the April 2026 org chart is **General Executive — of both
Pienissimo Srl and Pienissimo Software Srl**, alongside
[G Lanzetti](Giuliano%20Lanzetti%20-%20CEO%20of%20both%20Pienissimo%20companies.md) as CEO
of both. So she does have standing over the software company at the centre of
the scope dispute; she is not, however, its CEO.

She has attended only fragments of the tracked sessions — joining ~40 minutes
into one, ~16 minutes into another and leaving early. **Almost everything she
has "approved" reached her second-hand through Sabatino Rinaldi**, which is the
root of two live problems:

- she approved the phase 1 / phase 2 **timeline**, and that approval is being
  read as acceptance of scope and budget —
  [OI-83](../items/OI-83%20No%20phase%202%20estimate.md);
- the [phase 2 scope dispute](../risks/Risk%20-%20the%20phase%202%20scope%20dispute%20is%20unresolved.md)
  was escalated to her, and on 2026-08-06 Sabatino said plainly that
  _"Daniela non sapeva questa informazione qui."_

She also confirmed the one-bundle-per-order rule and validated the
signature-gated ticket flow.
