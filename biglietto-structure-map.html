Biglietto data relations

Relationship map restructured in the same card-and-connector format as the “Data relations” page, using the ticketing decisions confirmed in the meeting transcripts.

Solid orange lines are lookup relationships. Solid teal lines are parent/child links used to show the record hierarchy. Dashed gray lines are automation or sync flows, where the relation exists by process rather than as a direct field on the object. In this local project view, Biglietto has no direct Account field: buyer context stays indirect through the sales records.
